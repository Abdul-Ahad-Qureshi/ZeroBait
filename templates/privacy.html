Core Privacy Principles

Last revised: August 2026

1. Guest Analysis (Zero Account Requirement)

Any analyst or user can inspect target URLs and access real-time heuristic scoring without registering an account. Guest submissions are evaluated in ephemeral memory. Guest queries do not establish a persistent user profile and are not tied to any personal identifiers.

2. Isolation of Personal Scan Data

When authenticated under a registered account, your scan history is strictly associated with your unique account identifier (user_id). No user can ever query or view another user's personal inspection logs. Only anonymized global telemetry aggregates (such as total threat counts and TLD frequencies) are displayed on the public Live Threat Tracker.

3. Cryptographic Password Security

User credentials are encrypted using industry-standard bcrypt with salt hashing before database commitment. Plaintext passwords are never logged, transmitted, or accessible by our administrators.

4. Telemetry Sanitization & Defanging

When sharing or indexing indicators of compromise (IOCs), our engine automatically offers defanged formatting (e.g. hxxp://domain[.]com) to prevent accidental click-throughs or automated browser pre-fetching.

5. Right to Erasure (GDPR Article 17)

Logged-in analysts maintain complete sovereignty over their data. You may export your entire audit history as CSV or permanently purge all personal inspection logs directly from your Analyst Workspace at any time.
